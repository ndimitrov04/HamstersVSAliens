## Introduction

Aliens are attacking your planet!

Destroy them and save your hamster kingdom by crashing into their ships with your hamster ball and shooting them with various weapons. The more **momentum** you have, the higher you can jump and the more devastating abilities you can unleash upon your enemies.

## Gameplay

### 🏗️ Turrets & Beacons

You won't be alone in this fight! Build and upgrade:

- **Turrets** — Destroy enemy ships automatically
- **Beacons** — Protect your buildings or grant powerful AOE buffs

![Turrets and Beacons](https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzIyMTgyNTQ2LnBuZw==/original/DvcoJX.png)

### 🔧 Ball Upgrades & Weapons

Acquire powerful hamster ball upgrades and unlock a variety of weapons!

![Upgrades and Weapons](https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzIyMTgyNTU2LnBuZw==/original/VCSX6P.png)

### 👾 Enemies

Your enemies might get twice as powerful, but they won't catch you falling behind. Fight a multitude of enemy types:

| Enemy | Description |
| ------- | ------------- |
| **Seeker** | A small drone that follows you at all times, keeping you on your toes |
| **Carrier** | A large, powerful ship that carries many smaller ships |
| **Rusher** | A fast spaceship that can blow up and annihilate anything in its path |
| *...and many more* | |
